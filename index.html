—— KNOW THE WARNING SIGNS

Your Siding Usually Warns You
Before It Fails.

  • 01

    Cracks or Gaps

    Visible separations that invite moisture and compromise structural integrity.

  • 02

    Warping or Buckling

    Uneven boards indicating significant underlying moisture or thermal damage.

  • 03

    Moisture, Moss or Staining

    Trapped dampness leading to rot, mold, and degradation of materials.

  • 04

    Fading or Surface Breakdown

    Severe chalking or color loss signaling the protective coating has failed.
